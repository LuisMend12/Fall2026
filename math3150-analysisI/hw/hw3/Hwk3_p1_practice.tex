\textbf{Tools.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Triangle inequality: $|a+b|\le|a|+|b|$ for all $a,b\in\mathbb{R}$.
  \item $|-a| = |a|$.
  \item For $c\ge 0$: \quad $|a|\le c \iff -c\le a\le c$.
\end{itemize}

% ============================================================
\section*{Worked example}
\textbf{Prove:} for all $x,y\in\mathbb{R}$, \quad $|x|-|y|\le|x+y|$.

\textbf{Trick:} write $x$ as a sum that contains $x+y$:
\[
  x = (x+y) + (-y).
\]
Take absolute values and apply the triangle inequality:
\[
  |x| = |(x+y)+(-y)| \le |x+y| + |-y| = |x+y| + |y| .
\]
Subtract $|y|$ from both sides:
\[
  |x|-|y| \le |x+y| . \qquad \checkmark
\]

\section*{Practice C}
\textbf{Prove:} for all $x,y,z\in\mathbb{R}$,
\[
  |x-z| \le |x-y| + |y-z| .
\]

\emph{Hint:} what can you add to \emph{and} subtract from $x-z$ so it
splits into the two pieces on the right?

\vspace{3cm}

% ============================================================
\section*{The pattern}
In every one of these, you \textbf{rewrite a number as a sum of two
pieces}, where one piece is the quantity you want on the right-hand side.
Then apply $|a+b|\le|a|+|b|$ and rearrange. In HW3 Problem 1 that piece is
$x-y$; here it is $x+y$.
